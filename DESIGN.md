## Design Vision
A high-performance "Cyber-Audit" interface. The design uses flat, hyper-saturated neon accents against a deep architectural dark gray. High-contrast black borders (3px) and hard drop shadows (4px) create a physical, stenciled feel. 

## Layout
Strict 12-column grid for desktop, 4-column for mobile. No rounded corners. Typography is strictly divided between Montserrat (High-impact headlines) and Space Mono (Technical data and labels).
